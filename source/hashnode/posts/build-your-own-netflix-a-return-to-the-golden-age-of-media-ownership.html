This project is a journey that will not only give you the ultimate media server but also a solid foundation in modern IT skills.

The Magic Behind the Scenes: A Workflow That Works for You

At the heart of this system is a beautifully simple, yet powerful, automation loop. It's a series of applications that act as a digital butler for your media library. The process is so smooth that it feels like magic.

The journey starts with a simple request. You (or a family member) asks the system for a piece of media they want to watch. From there, the entire process is handled automatically:

  • The system receives the request and begins an intelligent search.

  • Once the content is found, the system coordinates the download, ensuring it's done securely and privately.

  • After the download is complete, the file is automatically sorted, renamed, and placed in the correct location.

  • Finally, the system notifies you that your requested media is ready to be streamed on any device.

It's a complete, end-to-end solution that operates with a level of efficiency a major corporation would envy.

The Tools That Make It Happen

This amazing workflow is built upon a foundation of powerful, open-source software. Each tool has a specific purpose and works harmoniously with the others.

  • Ombi: This is your public-facing portal. It's where you and your family can log in and effortlessly request a movie or a TV show.

  • The ARR Stack (Radarr, Sonarr, Lidarr): The brains of the operation. These are intelligent download managers that automatically search for, monitor, and acquire movies, TV shows, and music. They handle the hard work of finding the perfect version of your requested media.

  • Prowlarr/Jackett: These act as "indexers" for the ARR apps. They connect to various sources and help the ARR apps find the media they are looking for.

  • qBittorrent: A lightweight and powerful torrent client that handles the actual downloading of the content.

  • TrueNAS: The central nervous system of your storage. All your downloaded media is securely and reliably stored here, serving as the single source of truth for your entire library.

  • Jellyfin: Your personal streaming server. It’s an elegant, open-source alternative to Plex that can stream your content to any device, anywhere in the world.

  • Gotify/Discord/Email: Your personal notification system. You’ll get a friendly alert on your phone or computer as soon as your requested media is ready to watch!

  • NZB 360: A powerful mobile app for admins. It allows you to check on your system, approve requests, and troubleshoot issues, all from the palm of your hand.
