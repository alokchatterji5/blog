I built a little workshop to watch my AI agents work.

Once my agents were running, I realised I couldn't see what they were doing. A request goes in, an answer comes out, and everything between is a black box.

So I vibe coded a window into it: an animated, Tony Stark style workshop where each agent is a character and you can watch the hand-offs happen.

What I like about it:

⚡ It never calls an AI model, so it costs no tokens
👀 It only runs while someone has the page open
🔒 It's read-only, and it never sees message text
🎬 Very fast hand-offs are stretched on screen, with the real time shown next to them

It just reacts to what the agents already leave behind, so there's no extra model and no bill. It's a fun way to see who passed what to whom.

The idea was inspired by two open source projects, The Delegation by arturitu and Claw3D by iamlukethedev. I wrote my own to keep it tiny and free of any model key.

Build yours in whatever theme you like. A train station, a kitchen, a spaceship.
