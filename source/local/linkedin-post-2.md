Homelab Update:

Before AI touched a single service, we spent time on documentation — not as a nice-to-have, as the thing that actually makes this work.

A chat session doesn't hold project state. Close it and it's gone. So the project is organized into folders by topic, not by date, with a couple of files every AI reads first no matter which model shows up — plus trackers and a running log of what went wrong and what changed after.
