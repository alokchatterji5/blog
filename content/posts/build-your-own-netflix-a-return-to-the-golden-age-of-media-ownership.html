The short version

Streaming promised one subscription and delivered a dozen, with shows that vanish overnight. A self-hosted stack turns a request into a finished, sorted, streamable file with no manual steps: Ombi, the *Arr apps, qBittorrent behind a VPN, TrueNAS and Jellyfin. You own the library, and you pick up Docker, sysadmin and networking skills on the way.

Streaming services promised a single, simple replacement for cable. Today your shows are scattered across a dozen apps, each with its own subscription. A show's first season is on one service and new seasons are exclusive to another. Content can disappear without warning. You're paying, but you don't own anything. You're renting access.

The alternative is owning your media like a DVD collection, with the convenience of streaming: total control, true ownership, a personal experience. Call it digital independence.

What you get

  • Control and ownership. You decide what's in the library and it stays. No licensing agreements or deletions.
  • Convenience. Set it and forget it. No more searching, downloading and organizing by hand.
  • Skills. Docker, system administration (an old PC or a Raspberry Pi will do), networking and automation, all hands-on.

How a request travels

You or a family member asks for something to watch. The system searches, coordinates a secure and private download, sorts and renames the file into the right place, and tells you it's ready to stream on any device. Click a step below to see which tool does it.

The tools, one line each
  • Ombi: the request portal.
  • Radarr, Sonarr, Lidarr, Readarr: automatic search, monitoring and acquisition.
  • Prowlarr / Jackett: indexers for the *Arr apps.
  • qBittorrent: the downloader, with Surfshark VPN.
  • TrueNAS: storage, the single source of truth.
  • Jellyfin: streaming to any device.
  • Gotify / Discord / email: notifications.
  • NZB 360: mobile app for admins.

What's next

A self-hosted system is yours to extend. Ideas on my list:

  • Transcode on the fly: a tool like Tdarr to convert files so they play smoothly on any device.
  • Automated backups: back up the whole library to the cloud.
  • Sharing: share your library privately and securely with friends and family.
